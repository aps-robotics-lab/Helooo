1. Objective

1. Objective

Outmaneuver or disable the opposing robot within the designated combat arena.

A robot may be declared defeated if it becomes immobilised, exits the arena or meets another event-specific defeat condition.

2. Approved Systems

2. Approved Systems

Only mechanisms approved during technical inspection may be used.

Weapons or mechanisms that create unreasonable risk to participants, spectators or venue equipment are prohibited.

3. Arena Conduct

3. Arena Conduct

Teams must never attack outside the arena.

Deliberately targeting a person, controller, cable or safety equipment is prohibited.

Judges may pause or terminate a match for safety, arena damage or rule violations.

4. Safety

4. Safety

All robots must pass technical inspection.

Organisers may stop, isolate or remove an unsafe robot at any time.

Event-day safety instructions take precedence over general baseline requirements.

Read this event rule set with your entire team before selecting the arena.
